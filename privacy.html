Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy covers Sub Vitro Publisher ("the app"), the tool Sub Vitro uses to publish its own videos to its own accounts.

What it accesses

Only Sub Vitro's own accounts, after the channel owner signs in and authorises it: basic account information (the account's name and id), permission to upload videos and drafts, and the public counts for Sub Vitro's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares).

What it stores, and where

The sign-in tokens are kept in a file on the channel owner's own computer, readable only by that user. Post ids and counts are kept in the channel's own project files. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't collect data about viewers or other users, doesn't sell or share data, doesn't use cookies or analytics, and doesn't use any data for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking it makes the stored tokens useless; the files holding them are then deleted.
