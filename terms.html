These terms cover Sub Vitro Publisher ("the app"), the tool Sub Vitro uses to publish its own videos.

Who can use it

The app is used only by the owner of the Sub Vitro channel, on the Sub Vitro accounts. It isn't available to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

It uploads finished videos, captions and disclosures that Sub Vitro made, to Sub Vitro's own accounts, through each platform's official API, and reads those posts' public counts (views, likes, comments, shares). It doesn't post on anyone else's behalf and doesn't read other people's accounts.

Platform rules

Everything posted follows the rules of the platform it's posted to, including TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation, and YouTube's Terms of Service. Content made with AI is labelled as such.
