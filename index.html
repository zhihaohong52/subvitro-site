Sub Vitro

Sub Vitro makes short videos that explain AI news for people who don't work in tech: what happened, what the numbers say, and what most coverage gets wrong. Every video is built from primary sources, which are listed with it.

The narration is an AI voice and the music is AI-generated; each post says so.

Sub Vitro Publisher

Sub Vitro Publisher is the tool the channel uses to upload its own finished videos to its own accounts through the platforms' official APIs (TikTok's Content Posting API, YouTube's Data API). It isn't offered to anyone else.
